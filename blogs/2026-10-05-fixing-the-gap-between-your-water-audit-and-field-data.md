A water audit is only as trustworthy as the field records behind it, from leak surveys to meter tests. When those records sit in separate spreadsheets and apps, the number that reaches the utility director can rest on rough estimates. Connecting fieldwork to the water audit through a Field Operations Management platform gives directors a number solid enough to plan around while improving the consistency of water auditing.
