Environmental data management often breaks down at the handoff, where monitoring records pass through several hands before compliance review. Records reassembled in the office draw extra scrutiny during audits and permit renewals, and some gaps require another site visit to close. Capturing structure at the point of collection gives compliance leaders records they can defend from the start.
