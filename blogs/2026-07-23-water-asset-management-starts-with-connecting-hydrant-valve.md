Water utilities inspect hydrants, valves, and meters constantly, yet these water infrastructure records remain among the hardest to verify. GIS, CMMS, and field inspection software are often implemented separately, leaving records scattered across systems that don’t talk. Connecting those systems into one record keeps condition assessment data current and turns audit requests from a scramble into a routine pull.
