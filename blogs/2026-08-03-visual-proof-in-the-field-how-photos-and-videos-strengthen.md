Water utility asset inspections often lack reliable photographic evidence. That leaves organizations vulnerable during contractor disputes, regulatory audits, and insurance claims. Visual inspection software like Fulcrum addresses this gap by using automation to embed asset IDs, GPS locations, and timestamps into photos at the moment of capture.
