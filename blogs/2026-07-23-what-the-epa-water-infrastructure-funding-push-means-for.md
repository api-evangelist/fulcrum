The EPA is speeding up delivery of several major water infrastructure funding programs, including the Drinking Water and Clean Water State Revolving Funds, the WIFIA loan program, and the Emerging Contaminants grant. Together, these programs direct federal investment toward water systems and projects that protect water quality and public health. Faster funding means a faster-arriving wave of permitting, inspection, and reporting work for the environmental consulting firms that support water utility clients.
